# Centromere drive

```
Theory: Centromere drive
Original paper (year): Henikoff S, Ahmad K, Malik HS.
  2001. The Centromere Paradox: Stable Inheritance with
  Rapidly Evolving DNA. Science 293:1098-1102. DOI
  10.1126/science.1062939. That paper states the paradox
  and proposes that rapidly evolving centromeric
  components can cause incompatibilities. Malik HS,
  Henikoff S. 2001. Adaptive Evolution of Cid, a
  Centromere-Specific Histone in Drosophila. Genetics
  157:1293-1298. DOI 10.1093/genetics/157.3.1293 is the
  accompanying evidence from adaptive evolution of the
  centromeric histone. The 2002 one-page Nature News and
  Views is not the source. Akera et al. 2017 (DOI
  10.1126/science.aan0092) and Fishman and Saunders 2008
  (DOI 10.1126/science.1161406) are later experimental
  tests, not the 2001 statement.
One-sentence prediction: In female meiosis, larger or
  otherwise favoured centromeres exploit an asymmetric
  spindle to reach the egg; centromeric DNA and
  centromeric proteins then evolve in conflict, which
  can contribute to reproductive isolation.
Assumptions: an asymmetric meiosis (typically female);
  a functional difference among centromeres; a sexual
  generation in which meiosis occurs.
Which fail here: a grapevine cultivar kept by cuttings
  goes through no meiosis, so centromeres cannot drive
  in the clone. They can drive only in seedling
  generations of breeding. Centromeric differences among
  clones must arise in mitosis.
Modern data / statistic: telomere-to-telomere grapevine
  assemblies annotate centromeres (Shi et al. 2023, DOI
  10.1093/hr/uhad061). Among Pinot noir clones,
  structural variation is largely associated with
  repetitive DNA, including centromeric repeats (Callipo
  et al. 2026, DOI 10.1186/s13059-026-04184-x). These
  papers describe centromere landscapes and clonal
  structural variation; drive itself is not tested in
  grapevine here.
What would falsify it: in a system with asymmetric
  female meiosis, centromere-associated transmission
  bias is absent, or rapidly evolving centromeric
  proteins show no signature of conflict, after
  alternatives are taken into account.
What we will not claim: that the 2001 Science paper is a
  primary experimental test of drive. That Shi 2023 or
  Callipo 2026 demonstrated drive in grapevine. That
  cuttings can be a test of meiotic drive.
```
